Who We Are

Noble Shield Security Solutions

Noble Shield Limited is a UK-registered, SIA-licensed security company headquartered in Westcliff-on-Sea, Essex. Founded in 2026 with a clear mission: to deliver elite security services that genuinely protect people, assets and businesses across the UK.

We recognised a gap in the market for a security company that combines professional standards with genuine flexibility — one that doesn't lock clients into lengthy contracts but earns their loyalty through outstanding service every day.

Operating across Essex, London and Birmingham, Noble Shield provides manned guarding, mobile patrols, door supervision, event security, retail security and construction site protection — all delivered by fully vetted, DBS-checked SIA-licensed officers.

Our Standards

Every officer and every operation at Noble Shield Limited meets strict UK legal and industry standards.

✅
SIA Licensed Officers
All security personnel hold valid Security Industry Authority licences — a legal requirement for professional security work in the UK.
🔍
DBS Checked
Every officer has undergone a full Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) criminal record check before deployment.
📋
BS 7858 Vetted
All staff are screened to the BS 7858 British Standard for the vetting of security personnel — the gold standard for the industry.
