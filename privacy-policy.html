4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases as defined by UK GDPR: (a) Legitimate interests — to respond to your enquiries and provide quotations; (b) Contract performance — to deliver contracted security services; (c) Legal obligation — to comply with applicable UK law; (d) Consent — for marketing communications and non-essential cookies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We retain your personal information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Enquiry data is typically retained for 12 months. Contract-related data is retained for 7 years in line with HMRC requirements.
